# Summer of 2026

_May 15 — Aug 15 · 90 days · no excuses_

There are summers you forget immediately.

And then there are summers that split your life into before and after.

This one feels like the second kind.

For the next 90 days, I’m building three things in public:

- something that watches markets
- something that remembers
- something that chooses

That’s all I’ll say for now.

Outside of that:

Gym five times a week minimum.  
Reading more seriously than I ever have.  
Writing consistently.  
Learning to ship before I feel ready.

The goal is not productivity.

The goal is transformation.

I spent too much time this year thinking, planning, optimizing, researching, sketching systems I never fully committed to. This summer is different. The planning phase is over. I want motion. Output. Friction. Scar tissue.

So this is the structure:

Wake early.  
Train hard.  
Build every day.  
Publish everything worth publishing.  
Repeat until August.

No fake “grindset” mythology.  
No pretending balance doesn’t matter.  
No romanticizing burnout.

Just sustained, focused effort for 90 days.

Some things will fail publicly.  
Some ideas will turn out to be stupid.  
Some things might actually work.

That’s the point.

I’m especially interested in what happens when you compress ambition into a short enough timeframe that excuses stop fitting inside it.

Ninety days is long enough to build something real.  
Short enough to stay obsessed.

By the end of the summer, I want:

- code shipped
- systems built
- writing published
- stronger body
- clearer thinking
- better taste
- fewer doubts

And maybe, if things go well, the beginning of something much larger.

See you on the other side.
